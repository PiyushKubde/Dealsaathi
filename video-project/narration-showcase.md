# DealSaathi showcase — narration (about 3 minutes)

Follows the four required parts: the app, the viaSocket SDK integration, hosting on Embarko, and the
live result. Every claim is shown on screen or backed by the evidence in `asset-manifest.md`.
Read it in one take, with a short pause between parts. Replace [your name] or drop that sentence.

## Scene 1 — The app (0:00–0:45)

> Hi, I'm [your name], and this is DealSaathi: an AI-powered real estate workspace for agents, buyers and sellers.
> Buying, selling or renting a home usually means endless calls, messages and follow-ups. DealSaathi puts everyone in one place.
> Agents get one dashboard for their leads, listings, viewings and follow-ups, and they can add a lead just by describing it in plain words.
> Sellers list a property, and it's matched against every buyer's saved requirements.
> Buyers see their matches with the reasons, tap "I'm interested", and make an offer, which their agent forwards to the owner.

## Scene 2 — The integration, with the viaSocket SDK (0:45–1:30)

> Every integration in DealSaathi runs on the viaSocket SDK.
> I gave Claude Code viaSocket's setup prompt and three keys. It installed the SDK, and one client on the server gives the app both the AI and actions in each user's own apps.
> Each agent connects their own Gmail, Slack and Google Calendar through viaSocket's connect window, and viaSocket keeps the access, not DealSaathi.
> So viewings are booked straight into the agent's calendar, and alerts go out by email or Slack, without writing a single sign-in flow or API connector.

## Scene 3 — Hosting on Embarko (1:30–2:10)

> Hosting was just as quick. On Embarko, I clicked Host new app, copied the prompt, and pasted it into Claude Code.
> Claude Code packaged the app and deployed it. No Dockerfile and no server setup: under a minute later, it was live at its own address.
> Embarko's dashboard shows it running, with traffic, every deployment, and the scheduled job that sends follow-up reminders every fifteen minutes.
> Every update since has been one more prompt and one more deploy.

## Scene 4 — The live result (2:10–2:55)

> Here's DealSaathi, live.
> A seller lists a villa. A buyer finds it, taps "I'm interested", and offers eighty-eight lakh.
> The agent forwards the offer, the owner counters at ninety, and the buyer accepts in principle.
> Every step notifies the right people, and the buyer's identity stays private from the seller.

## Scene 5 — Close (2:55–3:10)

> That's DealSaathi: built with Claude Code, integrated with the viaSocket SDK, and hosted on Embarko.
> From the first prompt to a live app took about thirty-five minutes. If you're building your next app, give viaSocket and Embarko a try.
